# Until

Until is a personal tracker for expiration dates and shelf life after opening.

Keep track of food, cosmetics, medicine, pet products, and other everyday items. See what needs to be used soon and what has already expired.

## Features

- Add an item with just a name; add its date and other details later
- Track printed dates and “use within” periods after opening
- See expiration countdowns and items needing attention
- Organize items by category and location
- Track quantities and mark items as used or discarded
- Add product photos and photos of packaging labels
- Scan barcodes or get suggestions from text on a photo
- Search, filter, and sort your items
- Keep a history of used and discarded items
- Use the app offline and sync with an account when online
- Install the PWA on supported devices

Suggested names and dates from photos require your confirmation.

## Offline first

Items and photos are saved locally, so you can continue using Until without a connection. Changes made offline can sync when you reconnect.

## Notifications

Push notifications are not available yet. The app’s Soon view shows items approaching their expiration date.
